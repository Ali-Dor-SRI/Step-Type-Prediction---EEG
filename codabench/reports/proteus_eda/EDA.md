## 2. How fast a session's running reference settles

Distance from the running mean of the first n windows (stream order) to the session's full mean, relative to the distance of the other people's pooled mean (= the reference a model without re-centring uses). Median over the 14 sessions.

| n windows | 8 | 16 | 32 | 64 | 128 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| relative distance | 0.66 | 0.54 | 0.44 | 0.27 | 0.16 |

Below 1 = already closer to the session's own centre than the training mean is. Runs inside a session change paradigm (calibration runs 1–2, online runs 3–6) and interface, so a short buffer also follows within-session drift.

## Reading (for the model)

- **Every session is its own domain.** A session's mean covariance sits 5.3–9.9 from
  everyone else's pooled mean, and two sessions of the *same* person (P04, P10; median
  9.28) are about as far apart as two different people (median 9.97). Later sessions are
  therefore as hard as unseen people for a model without adaptation, which is why the
  per-stream re-centring (covariance +5.7, features +8.3 on lopo) carries the candidate.
- **The running reference settles fast.** After 16 windows it is already half as far from
  the session's own centre as the training mean is (0.54), after 64 windows a quarter (0.27):
  consistent with the buffer of 64 tying with longer buffers in Phase 1.
- **The class signal is frontal and slow.** The Fisher ratio is highest in frontal theta
  (AF4), then frontal alpha; central (motor) channels come second. The three tasks
  (motor imagery, mental subtraction, word association) differ in frontal theta, the
  classic working-memory signature, **but frontal channels also carry eye movements**,
  which can differ between a calculation and a word task. Part of the separable signal may
  be ocular. It counts for the competition score; it should not be described as purely
  cortical. A check would need the raw EOG (not in the cache).
- Separability is a little higher in the calibration runs (alpha 0.087) than in the online
  runs (0.066): feedback does not add class information on this measure.
