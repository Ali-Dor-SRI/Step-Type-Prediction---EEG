# A3: why is P04 bad? (B3 = X4f' + nets, w 0.6; LOPO; script analysis/sprint1114_p04.py, output logs/sprint_1114/a3_p04.out)

1. Accuracy. P04 B3 bacc 0.533 (others 0.60-0.86, mean 0.743). Components: X4f 0.522, X4f' 0.522, nets 0.492 -> every part is near chance (0.333 is chance; 0.5 is weak).
   - Per session: s1 0.618, s2 0.486, s3 0.495. P04 is the only person with 3 sessions besides P10 (s1 0.59 / s2 0.74 / s3 0.65). Others (1 session) 0.60-0.86.
   - Per run: 0.20-0.77, no run-wise pattern (s1 r1/r3 0.76, s3 r4 0.20); noisy (n=30 each).
   - Confusion (rows true mi/sub/word): [[98,25,65],[44,68,76],[35,19,136]]. Over-predicts word (277 vs 190 true), under-predicts sub (112 vs 188); sub recall 0.36, mi 0.52, word 0.72.
2. Signal quality: NOT the cause. No windows >5x global median RMS (P05 3.7%, P06 2.1%, P04 0%); no flat channels (any person); P04 p99 |x| 251 uV (typical); line noise 50 Hz ratio 1.6 (P02/P08 5+, fine). Window RMS median 16.5 uV (lowest group). Only oddity: frontal channels quieter than the cohort (AF4 z -1.56, F5 -1.37, AF3 -1.35, F3 -1.27, F4 -1.23, Fz -1.20 on log-RMS); P95/P5 channel-RMS spread 1.4 (smallest = most uniform).
3. Covariance shift: no outlier. Log-Euclid distance of P04 person-mean to training mean 4.64 (others 4.44-7.19; P06 4.44 and P09 4.63 are close and score 0.77/0.80). Window p90 10.19 (others 9.3-12.4). Spearman(dist, bacc) = +0.39, p 0.26. Within-person session-to-session shift for P04 7.6/5.6/7.8 is the same as P10 (6.5/8.8/8.0).
4. Pseudo-labels: PL (0.5 Px + 0.5 Pn) acc on P04 = 0.524 bacc (others 0.59-0.85); acc on top-85% confident = 0.551 (others 0.60-0.89), first 40 windows 0.70 (n=40, noise), after 40 windows 0.511. Mean PL max-prob 0.584 (lowest-but-P05 0.587; P08 0.753). So ~45% of pseudo-labels are wrong and confidence barely separates right from wrong: self-training has nothing to learn from (explains zero adaptation gain: X4f' = X4f = 0.522). PL confusion is identical to the B3 confusion (the labels are the predictions).
5. Actionable (label-free, causal):
   - Artefact rejection/channel drop: nothing to reject (0-10 flagged windows; flagged acc 0.10 at k=2 but n=10, k=3 n=2 -> no effect possible, <0.2% of windows). Channel drop: no bad channel found.
   - Causal running class-balance bias correction (subtract a * running mean of past log-probs): P04 0.533 -> 0.539 (a=0.5) / 0.530 (a=1); cohort mean 0.722 -> 0.716 / 0.716. NO GAIN, slight loss for others.
   - Verdict: P04 is a hard/low-SNR subject for this feature+net family (low-confidence everywhere, sessions 2-3 at chance level 0.49), not a data-quality or covariance-outlier problem. No label-free arm with expected gain found; expected effect of any of the above on cohort mean <= +0.0005 (1 of 10 people, <=+0.005 on P04).
   - Caveat: P04 session 1 (0.62) vs sessions 2-3 (0.49) hints at within-person drift, but covariance-shift magnitude is not unusual, so covariance re-centring per session (already in X4f/AdaBN) is unlikely to be the missing piece.
